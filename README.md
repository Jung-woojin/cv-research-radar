# CV Research Radar

Automated research intelligence archive for Computer Vision, VLM, multimodal learning, open-source releases, research ideas, Japanese CV labs, and biweekly trend maps.

## Archive structure

- `daily/YYYY/MM/YYYY-MM-DD.md` — CV/DL daily research briefing
- `daily/benchmarks/YYYY/MM/YYYY-MM-DD.md` — Daily Benchmark Movement
- `daily/failures/YYYY/MM/YYYY-MM-DD.md` — Daily Failure / Negative Result Watch
- `daily/questions/YYYY/MM/YYYY-MM-DD.md` — Daily Research Question Generator
- `ideas/YYYY/MM/YYYY-MM-DD.md` — CV research idea radar
- `japan-labs/YYYY/MM/YYYY-MM-DD.md` — Japan CV lab watch
- `opensource/YYYY/MM/YYYY-MM-DD.md` — CV open-source radar
- `trends/YYYY/MM/YYYY-MM-DD.md` — biweekly CV trend map

Reports are generated by scheduled ChatGPT research tasks and archived automatically.

## Research workflow

The full research requirements are in [docs/research-radar-workflow.md](docs/research-radar-workflow.md); repository execution rules are in [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md). Existing archive paths are preserved. New reports require verified primary sources and duplicate checks before a direct Markdown write, commit, and push. Historical placeholders are not treated as completed research.

Preferred schedule (Asia/Seoul): daily benchmarks at 05:30, failures at 06:30, questions at 07:30, and weekly open-source radar on Monday at 08:00. The target is completion before 09:00. Scheduling is managed in the Codex app, outside Git; these times alone do not configure a scheduler.
